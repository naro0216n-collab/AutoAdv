The repository documents this artifact as a tf2onnx conversion of Google's
YAMNet (google/yamnet/1), with no retraining or weight changes. YAMNet predicts
AudioSet event classes. The model is distributed under Apache License 2.0;
AudioSet ontology labels are provided by Google under CC BY 4.0.

The model is downloaded into the user's managed AutoAdv runtime and is not
committed into this source repository.
